How Nigiri picks your cards

The four ratings

Again
you forgot.
Hard
right, but slow or unsure. It still counts as remembered.
Good
right, with some effort.
Easy
right, with no effort.

When a card comes back

Tap Again and the card comes back after 1 minute. Hard on a card you're still learning waits 90 seconds.

Every other wait is at least a day. Nigiri works it out from how you've answered that card before, aiming for you to remember about 9 in 10 cards when they come back.

Easy aims a little higher, about 19 in 20. So an Easy card still waits longer than Good would, just not as long as it could.

There's no daily limit. Review as much as you like.

The maths is called FSRS, if you're curious.

With a test date

Cards not studied yet are spread over the days before the test, so each one comes up at least a day early.

Take a day off, and the rest spread out over the days left.

Move the date, and only that plan changes. Cards you've studied keep their schedule: a test never moves them.
